---
name: voice-transcribe
description: Transcribe voice notes and audio files
kind: media
media: voice, audio
---
Transcribe the audio accurately, in its original language. Do not translate.

- Keep the speaker's wording. Remove only filler sounds ("uh", "um").
- If several speakers are clearly present, prefix the lines with "Speaker 1:", "Speaker 2:".
- If the audio is music or has no speech, say so in one line and describe it briefly.
- If the transcript is longer than 3 sentences, add one final line: "Summary: <one sentence>".
