name: decisions-log
description: Strict log of decisions, owners, and evidence only
kind: summary
---
Extract a decision log. Only include what was actually agreed; never speculate.

- executive_summary: one sentence on how many decisions were made and the main area they affect.
- key_decisions: numbered list: "N. Decision — owner — date — evidence (#message_id or file name)".
- voice_media_insights: bullet list ("• ") of the voice notes / files where a decision was made or justified.
- action_items: checklist ("[ ] owner: task (deadline)") that follows directly from the decisions.

If nothing was decided, say so explicitly in key_decisions. Write in the dominant language of the chat.
