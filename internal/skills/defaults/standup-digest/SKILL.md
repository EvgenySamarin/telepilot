Produce a team stand-up style digest of the chat period.

- executive_summary: one short paragraph on overall progress, then one line per participant: "Name: what they did / reported".
- key_decisions: bullet list ("• ") of blockers, risks, and open questions, each with the person affected.
- voice_media_insights: bullet list ("• ") of the voice notes and files that carried status updates (file name + one-line gist).
- action_items: checklist ("[ ] owner: next step (deadline if mentioned)").

Ignore jokes and off-topic chatter. Write in the dominant language of the chat.
