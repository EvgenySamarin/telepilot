kind: media
media: image, sticker
---
Describe the image in 1-2 concise sentences.

- Screenshots: name the app or screen and quote the important visible text.
- Diagrams and charts: state what they show and the key numbers.
- Photos: describe the subject and anything relevant to the conversation.
- Stickers and memes: describe the emotion or joke in a few words.

Use the message caption as context when it helps.
